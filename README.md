# Songbird

Learn piano songs with a moving score, an on-screen keyboard and illustrated hands that show which fingers to use.

[Open Songbird](https://chris-kc-cheng.github.io/songbird/)

![Latest Songbird screenshot](assets/screenshots/latest.png)

## Start practicing

1. Choose a song from **Song library** on the left. **Für Elise** is selected when you open the app.
2. Press **Play** to hear the song and follow the highlighted notes and keys. Press it again to pause.
3. Use **Restart** to return to the beginning, or **Next note** to step through the music.
4. Adjust **Tempo** to practice at a comfortable pace. Changing tempo pauses playback; press Play when ready.

Click any piano key to hear it. You can also press **Space** to play or pause while you are outside a text field or control. Songs stop at the end.

## Read the notes and hands

Use **Staff**, **123** and **ABC** above the score to show standard notation, numbered notation or note names. You can combine these views.

The illustrated hands show finger numbers:

| Finger | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Thumb | 1 |
| Index | 2 |
| Middle | 3 |
| Ring | 4 |
| Little | 5 |

Colored notes and keys show what is sounding. Choose **Hide hands** for an unobstructed view of the keyboard.

Fingerings are suggested practice guidance and may differ from your teacher's or printed score's choices. Keep your hands relaxed and move them when needed; an octave reach is not a reason to hold a stretched hand.

## Keep time

Turn **Metronome** on to hear a steady tick-tock at the selected tempo. It keeps ticking when you pause, restart or switch songs, until you turn it off. In 3/8 time, it marks eighth-note beats.

## Explore the song library

Search for a tune or composer, or filter by **Melodies** and **Chords**.

- Familiar tunes include **Ode to Joy**, **Hot Cross Buns**, **Mary Had a Little Lamb** and **Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star**.
- Classical themes include simplified excerpts of **Beethoven's Symphony No. 5**, **Vivaldi's Spring** and **Brahms' Lullaby**.
- **First chords**, **Together in C** and **C scale & octave chords** help you practice both hands, scales and wider intervals.
- **Für Elise** includes the full piece and its repeats in the original pitches. It opens at this edition's tempo of 72 BPM; lower the tempo whenever you need more time. The score link beside the playback controls opens the original public-domain sheet music.

## Bring your own song

Choose **Import song** at the bottom of the library, select a Songbird JSON file or paste its contents, then choose **Load song**. The import dialog includes example downloads. Imported songs remain available for the current session and are cleared when you refresh.

## Use Songbird on your device

The wide layout puts songs on the left, the score and keyboard in the middle, and practice controls on the right. On smaller screens, scroll sideways to reach the full layout.

You can install Songbird using your browser's install option when available. After your first online visit, the app and included songs are available offline. For sound, press Play or click a piano key.
